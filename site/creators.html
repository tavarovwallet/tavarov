NoN Wallet is a self-custody wallet. Keep your recovery phrase safe: it gives access to your funds. Tavarov Pay is a separate payment service; customers can pay from compatible wallets. Wallet and token support varies by network. Blockchain network fees apply.

Tavarov Pay’s current service fee is 1%. On EVM networks, the contract owner can change it up to 2%. The contracts have not been independently audited.
