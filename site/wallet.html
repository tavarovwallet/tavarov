Before you start.

Are NoN Wallet and Tavarov Pay the same product?

They are separate products. NoN Wallet manages your crypto. Tavarov Pay helps businesses accept payments. You can access the Pay dashboard through NoN Wallet, while customers can pay from other compatible wallets.

What does the wallet cost?

The wallet is free to open. Blockchain network fees apply. Tavarov Pay payments have a 1% service fee paid by the seller. Wallet swaps and cross-network transfers include a 0.5% project fee; review the complete quote before confirming.

Can you restore my wallet?

No. We cannot restore your wallet without the recovery phrase or a supported backup. Save your recovery phrase offline and never share it with support or enter it on another website.
