Questions

Can I use DonationAlerts and Tavarov at the same time?

Yes. They are separate links and separate OBS sources, so they do not interfere. Many streamers offer several ways to tip.

How much do I get from a 10 USDT tip?

9.9 USDT straight to your wallet. The contract keeps 1%; there is no payout or any other fee on our side. The viewer separately pays the network fee — a variable amount.

Who controls the received funds?

We cannot: the money goes from the viewer's wallet straight to yours and never passes through us. Only you control it.

How do I turn USDT into local currency?

Through an exchange or exchanger that works in your country. We do not cash out. Check your country's rules on crypto.

Current service fee: 1%. Network fees are separate. EVM fee settings may be changed up to the 2% contract limit; read the fee terms.
