Questions

What does this checker check?

It finds the transaction on the blockchain and reads it: whether it succeeded, when, how many confirmations it has, and every token transfer in it. Then it compares each token’s contract address with the official USDT and USDC contracts for that network. If the buyer sent a link, it also checks that the link is to a real explorer.

Which networks does it support?

BNB Chain (BEP20), Ethereum (ERC20) and TRON (TRC20). It checks USDT on all three and USDC on BNB Chain and Ethereum. Circle no longer supports USDC on TRON, so it is not counted as USDC. On BNB Chain, USDT and USDC are the Binance-Peg tokens that wallets and exchanges use there.

Do you see or store what I paste?

No. The check runs in your browser. It sends the transaction hash only to public blockchain nodes (Binance, BNB Chain, PublicNode, dRPC, 1RPC, TronGrid, TronStack), read-only, and nothing to us. Your address and amount never leave the page. No cookies, no trackers.

Why check the contract and not the token name?

Anyone can create a token and call it USDT, with the same logo. Only the contract address can’t be copied. A token named “USDT” with a different contract is not USDT, whatever the wallet or the explorer shows as its name.
