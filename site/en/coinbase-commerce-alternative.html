Coinbase Commerce closed. Same model, still open.

On March 31, 2026 Coinbase Commerce shut down for merchants outside the United States and Singapore, and its WooCommerce plugin is no longer available. Tavarov Pay works the way Commerce did: non-custodial, 1%, money straight to your own wallet, in any country.

Switch in five minutes

  1. Install Tavarov Pay for WooCommerce from the official WordPress.org directory.
  2. Paste the wallet address where you want to receive payments. NoN Wallet is free, but any wallet on the supported networks works.
  3. Turn off the old Coinbase Commerce payment method so customers don’t see a dead button.

Not on WooCommerce? Create invoices with one API call and get a signed invoice.paid webhook, or send a payment link in any chat. Docs and test mode are in the developer area.

Why non-custodial matters. A custodial gateway can freeze your balance, change its countries or close your account, and your money is stuck until it pays you out. With Tavarov Pay the customer’s payment is split on-chain in one transaction: 99% to your wallet, 1% to the service. If we disappeared tomorrow, every payment you already received would still be in your wallet.

Questions

Is this the same model as Coinbase Commerce?

Yes in the part that mattered: payments go straight to your own wallet and the fee is 1%. We never hold your money, so there is nothing to withdraw and nothing that can be frozen on our side.

Do you accept Bitcoin and Ether like Coinbase Commerce did?

No. Tavarov Pay accepts stablecoins only: USDT and USDC (USDT0 on X Layer). Your prices stay in dollars and you never take crypto price risk.

Do I need an account or KYC?

No account and no identity check are needed to receive payments to your own wallet. You are still responsible for following the laws and tax rules of your country.

Which countries are supported?

Tavarov Pay is software that works in any country where you may legally accept crypto payments. Some countries restrict crypto payments, so check the rules where you live.
