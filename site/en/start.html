Nobody in the middle

The current payment split is 99% to the seller and 1% to Tavarov Pay, in the same transaction. We do not hold a merchant balance or operate a payout schedule. Network fees are separate.

Questions

What does it cost?

Tavarov Pay currently charges 1% per payment, deducted from the seller’s proceeds. Blockchain network fees are separate and vary with the network and demand. On BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base, the contract owner can change the service fee up to the on-chain maximum of 2%. See the Terms for details.

How do I connect my store?

Connect through the API dashboard using a wallet signature, or create a payment link. You remain responsible for your sales, refunds, taxes and compliance with the rules that apply to your business.

What can the buyer pay with?

A wallet compatible with the chosen network and token, such as MetaMask, Trust Wallet, OKX, Phantom, Solflare or NoN Wallet. Supported networks are BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base (USDC only) and Solana.

What if the buyer wants a refund?

Blockchain payments cannot be cancelled by Tavarov Pay. The seller handles refunds from their own wallet. Available refund tools depend on the payment network and integration. The service fee is not refunded.
