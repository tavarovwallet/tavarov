Updated: 30 Oct 2026 · Tavarov Pay · NoN Wallet · API · WooCommerce plugin

In short. We make software: a wallet, payment pages, an API and a plugin. We never hold money or keys — payments go straight to the recipient on BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base and Solana. Each payment carries a 1% fee. What you do with your money, and following the laws of your country, is your responsibility.

1. The service

Tavarov ("we") provides the NoN Wallet app, payment, invoice and tip pages at wallet.tavarov.com, the Tavarov Pay API (wallet.tavarov.com/api/v1), the developer cabinet and the WooCommerce plugin (together, the "Service"). On BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base payments are executed by smart contracts; on Solana, by a single transfer transaction signed by the payer: 99% to the recipient, 1% to Tavarov. Contract addresses are published on the website; the source code of the BNB Chain contract is verified on BscScan.

2. We never hold your money or keys

NoN Wallet is non-custodial: keys are created and stored on your device. We cannot recover a lost recovery phrase or cancel or reverse a transfer. A contract payment sends the money to the recipient in one transaction; there is no balance "with us".

3. Fees

Each payment through Tavarov Pay carries a 1% fee, taken automatically in the same transaction. The network fee is paid by whoever sends the transaction; on Ethereum it is noticeably higher than on the others. On BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base the fee is set in the contract; the contract owner may change it, but never above 2% — that limit is written into the contract itself. On Solana the fee is set by the payment page. We will announce any change on the website.

Swaps and cross-network transfers in NoN Wallet are carried out by third-party services (swap aggregators and the Relay bridge). On top of their price the wallet charges 0.5% of the amount for the project. The total, including this 0.5%, is shown before you confirm.

  • Do not use the Service for fraud, money laundering, prohibited goods or services, or to deceive buyers or viewers.
  • Keep your recovery phrase, passwords, API keys and webhook secrets private.
  • Check addresses and amounts before sending: blockchain transactions are irreversible.
  • Sellers must meet their obligations to buyers, including refunds.

5. API and developer cabinet

An API key lets you create invoices for the wallet you signed in with. Requests are rate-limited. We may disable a key or cabinet used to break these terms or the law, or that disrupts the Service or other users. Webhooks are retried but delivery is not guaranteed — check the invoice status through the API before shipping goods (the WooCommerce plugin does this for you).

6. Refunds

A blockchain payment cannot be cancelled. Only the recipient can send money back — from their own wallet, including the refund button in NoN Wallet for contract payments. The 1% fee is not returned on refunds.

7. TVR points

Contract payments used to earn TVR bonus points. Since October 2026 no new points are credited; points credited earlier can be claimed in NoN Wallet under the contract's rules. TVR is not an investment or money: we do not sell it, we promise no price or return, and an exchange may never appear.
