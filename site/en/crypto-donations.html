What creators get

OBS alerts

A transparent Browser source: name, amount and message with a sound. The message appears only after payment, so nobody can put anything on your stream for free.

Goal bar

"New microphone 184 / 300 $" for OBS and on the tip page, counted from on-chain payments.

On-chain payments

A blockchain tip cannot be pulled back through a bank or PayPal a week later. Check the confirmed invoice before fulfilling the order.

How to set it up

  1. Install NoN Wallet and choose Creator mode.
  2. The Pay tab shows your tip link. Claim a short name for a clean link: /d/yourname.
  3. Copy the OBS alerts link: Sources → + → Browser, width 800, height 400.
  4. Write a greeting and a goal — viewers see them on your tip page.

The honest downside. Viewers need a wallet with USDT or USDC on one of four networks — BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base or Solana — so crypto tips work best as an extra button next to your usual ones.

Questions

What is the fee?

1% of each tip, taken in the same transaction; 99% goes straight to the creator. The viewer also pays the network fee: a separate amount that varies by network and demand.

What is the minimum tip?

10 cents. Smaller amounts are not shown on stream.

Can someone put a message on stream for free?

No. A message is shown only after a USDT or USDC payment confirmed on-chain.

Does the viewer need to sign up?

No. They open the link and pay from a compatible wallet: MetaMask, Trust Wallet, OKX, Phantom, NoN Wallet.

Current service fee: 1%. Network fees are separate. EVM fee settings may be changed up to the 2% contract limit; read the fee terms.
