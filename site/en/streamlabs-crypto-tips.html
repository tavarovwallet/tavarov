Questions

Will your alerts work together with Streamlabs alerts?

Yes. It is a separate Browser source in OBS and does not overlap with Streamlabs widgets.

What is a chargeback and why is there none in crypto?

A chargeback is when a payer disputes a card payment through their bank and the money is taken back from the streamer. A blockchain payment cannot be reversed, so that risk does not exist.

How much do I get from a 10 USDT tip?

9.9 USDT straight to your wallet. The viewer separately pays the network fee — a variable amount.
