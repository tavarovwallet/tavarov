Partner program

Bring a seller, a shop or a streamer — and for a whole year you get 20% of our fee on every payment they take. The contract pays you automatically, in the same transaction as the payment: no payout requests, no waiting.

Get your link in TelegramLink in NoN Wallet

In the bot — the /ref command. In NoN Wallet — the Partners tab → Invite a seller.

How much

Our fee is 1% of each payment. Your share is a fifth of it, which is 0.2% of the turnover of everyone you bring.

$1,000 a month

A seller you brought with that turnover — $2 a month for you.

$10,000 a month

A café or a small shop — $20 a month for you.

20 such sellers

At $10,000 a month each — $400 a month for you, for a year.

How it works

  1. You share a link: for Telegram — t.me/tavarov_pay_bot?start=r_your_address, for websites and social media — wallet.tavarov.com/ref?by=your_address.
  2. The seller opens it and confirms you with one signature — in NoN Wallet, MetaMask, Trust Wallet or another wallet.
  3. Every payment they take through Tavarov Pay — invoices, checkout, tips, the bot, the WooCommerce plugin or the API — is split by the contract: 99% to the seller, and a fifth of our 1% straight to you. This works on all four networks: BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base and Solana. If the seller takes payments on several networks, they confirm you on each.
  4. Who you brought and how much you earned — in the bot (/ref) and in NoN Wallet.

The seller pays no more

We share our fee, not their revenue. Prices for customers stay the same.

No switching later

The seller confirms a partner themselves, once and before their first sale. Nobody can change it afterwards, not even us.

Open on-chain

On BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base the rule lives in an open contract; on Solana your share goes in the same payment transaction. Every payout is a separate record in the network explorer.

Who it is for

  • Streamers — set up crypto tips for yourself and invite fellow streamers.
  • Telegram channel admins writing about business, freelancing and crypto.
  • Web developers — install the WooCommerce plugin for a client and earn a share of their sales for a year.
  • Anyone who knows sellers: cafés, craftspeople, tutors, social-media shops.

Do not promise people earnings, pretend to be Tavarov, or send spam. The partner share is payment for sellers you bring, not an investment.

Questions

How much will I get?

20% of our fee — 0.2% of the turnover of each seller you bring — for 365 days from the moment they confirm you.

When is the money paid?

Immediately, in the same transaction as the customer payment. The contract sends your share straight to your wallet in USDT or USDC — there is nothing to withdraw.

What if the seller already took payments?

Then a partner can no longer be set: the contract only allows it before the first sale.

Can I invite myself?

No, the contract does not allow it.

Where do I see my earnings?

In the Telegram bot with /ref and in NoN Wallet on the Partners tab. Every payout is also visible in the network explorer (BscScan, Etherscan, BaseScan, Solscan) as an incoming transfer to your wallet.

Current service fee: 1%. Network fees are separate. EVM fee settings may be changed up to the 2% contract limit; read the fee terms.
