Why digital sellers

A clear payment status

The invoice page shows confirmation after an on-chain payment is verified. Check the server-side payment status before delivering a digital product.

Funds go to your wallet

Tavarov Pay does not hold a merchant balance or schedule payouts. Access to the API may be restricted under the Terms; blockchain networks and token issuers have their own rules.

Use a compatible wallet

Connect the receiving wallet you control. NoN Wallet is optional; customers can use other compatible wallets on the selected network.

Understand the fees

The current Tavarov Pay fee is 1%. Blockchain network fees are separate and vary. On BNB Chain, Ethereum and Base, the contract owner can change the fee up to 2%; see the Terms.

How a sale goes

1

You make an invoice. Amount, currency, what it is for. You get a link and a QR code.

2

You send the link. In chat, in email, on your site — wherever you already talk to buyers.

3

The customer pays with a wallet compatible with the selected network and token. Confirmation time depends on the network.

4

You see the payment. The invoice page and your invoice log show “Paid” as soon as the network confirms it.

Selling through a website? Use the Tavarov Pay API or WooCommerce plugin. Verify the invoice status on your server before delivering goods.

Honestly

  • The contracts have no independent audit. We checked them ourselves, many times, and the checks are public — but that is not the same thing. Start small.
  • Rules differ by country. In some places paying with crypto for goods and services is restricted or forbidden. Check the rules where you are registered.
  • Refunds remain the seller’s responsibility. A blockchain payment cannot be cancelled by Tavarov Pay. Refund tools vary by network; you may need to send a separate transfer.
  • TVR points are not an investment. They have no price and are not for sale. Anyone offering to sell you TVR is a fraud.
